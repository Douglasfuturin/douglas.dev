# Transition Phrases

> The bridge language that moves the audience between sections without breaking flow. Stolen from years of watching Brunson, Hormozi, Frank Kern, Sam Ovens, and Stefan Georgi present.

These are speaker phrases, what [VOICE OWNER] says aloud as they advance to the next slide. They appear in speaker notes on the deck, not on the slide itself (unless the slide pattern is `bridge` or `mono-line`).

Adapt them to [VOICE OWNER]'s natural cadence. The patterns work; the literal words shouldn't be copied (audience pattern-recognition).

## Cold open → Who Am I (slide 8)

- "Quick intro on me then we dive straight in."
- "Before we get into it, let me tell you who's talking to you."
- "30 seconds on background, then content."

## Who Am I → Big Domino (slide 20)

- "But here's why you're really here."
- "Now, the reason I'm sharing this..."
- "Okay, let's get to the stuff that actually matters."

## Big Domino → Origin Story (slide 30)

- "Let me show you how I figured this out."
- "The story of how I learned this is kind of unbelievable."
- "I want to walk you back to where this started."

## Origin Story → Secret 1 (slide 50)

- "And it all came down to three things I had to figure out."
- "From that moment, everything was about three secrets."
- "What I learned next is what I want to show you today, three secrets."

## Between secrets (50→51, 75→76, 95→96)

**Into Secret 1:**
- "Secret #1 is about the vehicle itself."
- "First, we have to talk about whether this even works."

**Secret 1 → Secret 2:**
- "Okay so Secret #1 is the WHAT. But here's what trips most people up..."
- "That's the method. But there's a second thing, and this one's harder."
- "You can know the method and still not do it. Here's why..."

**Secret 2 → Secret 3:**
- "So that's the internal block. But there's one more thing in the way."
- "Even if you've got the method and the right mindset, the world will still get in the way. Let me show you what I mean."

## Secret 3 → Transition (slide 110)

- "So now you've got all three secrets."
- "If you take only those three things from today, you'll move further than 95% of people in this space."
- "That's the WHAT. Now let's talk about something else."

## Transition / Permission (slides 111-115)

The permission ritual itself. These ARE the slide content for slides 111-114.

- 111: "What we just covered is the WHAT."
- 112: "But I bet you're wondering about the HOW."
- 113: "Would it be okay if I showed you the system I built for this?"
- 114: [black slide, wait]
- 115: "Okay, here we go."

**After permission, the bridge phrasings:**
- "What I'm about to show you is the result of [X years / $X spent / Y failures]."
- "This is what I wish someone had handed me [N years ago]."
- "I built this because I got tired of [problem]."

## Offer Reveal → Stack (slide 125)

- "Now let me show you everything you get..."
- "Here's the full picture..."
- "Let me walk you through what's included..."

## Stack → Total (slide 133→134)

- "If you add all of that up..."
- "When you look at what you're actually getting..."
- "The total value of everything you're getting..."

## Total → Price (slides 134→137)

- "Now I know what you're thinking..."
- "You're expecting me to charge what this is actually worth..."
- "But you're not going to pay anywhere close to that..."
- "You're not even going to pay half of that..."
- "Today, you can get all of this for..."

## Price → Closes (slide 137→141)

- "Now let me show you why this is a no-brainer..."
- "Let me show you the math..."
- "I want you to see exactly what this means in dollar terms..."

## Money → Risk (slide 142→143)

- "And here's the thing, you can't lose."
- "I'm going to take all the risk off the table..."
- "Even if you don't believe me, here's what I'll do..."

## Risk → Time (slide 144→145)

- "But here's why you need to decide today..."
- "There's a reason I'm only doing this right now..."
- "Let me tell you why this isn't going to be open forever..."

## Time → Action (slide 146→147)

- "Here's exactly what to do right now..."
- "If this is for you, here's the next move..."
- "Three steps and you're in..."

## Action → CTA (slide 147→148)

- "Go to [URL] right now."
- "Click the button below."
- "Hit the link and let's get you started."

## Holding the CTA during FAQ

- "I'll be here to answer questions while you grab your spot."
- "While I take questions, the link is right there on the screen."
- "Any final questions? The link is up, I'll take a few while people register."

## Closing the webinar

- "Whichever way you decide, thank you for being here."
- "I'll see you on the other side."
- "Either way, I'm rooting for you. Go do the work."

## Pattern fatigue warnings

These phrases worked because audiences hadn't heard them 50 times. By 2026, several have become recognizable Brunson/Hormozi tells:

- "Would it be okay if...", pure Brunson, instant recognition
- "Let me show you the math...", Hormozi tell
- "Here's the thing...", overused universally
- "What this means is...", overused
- "If I could show you... would you...", pure Brunson conditional

For [VOICE OWNER]'s authentic voice, REPLACE these with [VOICE OWNER]'s natural bridges. Read [VOICE OWNER]'s podcasts / past webinars and harvest the actual phrases they use to pivot. Those will work better than the textbook ones.
