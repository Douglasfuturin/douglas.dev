# Subject Line Swipes — by sequence type

Proven subject line patterns. Use as starting points, not templates — every subject still gets voice-checked against [VOICE OWNER]'s past performers and the Voice Bible. The pattern is the seed; the voice is the soul.

## Universal rules

- **≤ 7 words.** Shorter wins. Mobile previews cut at ~30 characters.
- **No emojis unless the Voice Bible explicitly approves.** They tend to scream "marketing" — most premium brands shouldn't.
- **No ALL CAPS.** Triggers spam filters and reads like shouting.
- **No deceptive subjects.** "RE:" or "Fwd:" when there's no thread = trust collapse on open.
- **No banned subjects.** Check `brains/email/banned-subjects.md` before using anything from this file.

## Welcome sequence

**Email 1 (Confirm + identity):**
- `Welcome — here's your {lead magnet name}`
- `Read this first`
- `You're in. Quick thing.`
- `{first_name}, here's that thing you wanted`

**Email 2 (Origin story):**
- `Why I started doing this`
- `The {industry} mistake I kept making`
- `True story`
- `Something I haven't told many people`

**Email 3 (Big idea):**
- `Most {industry} advice is backwards`
- `The thing nobody talks about`
- `What I actually believe about {topic}`
- `Why {common belief} is wrong`

**Email 4 (Proof):**
- `{Name} went from {A} to {B} in {timeframe}`
- `Here's what happened when {name} tried it`
- `{Specific number} in {timeframe}`
- `Real story, real numbers`

**Email 5 (Soft pitch):**
- `If any of this resonated...`
- `Here's where we go from here`
- `Want to keep going?`
- `What's next`

## Nurture sequence

**Story → lesson:**
- `Something happened {timeframe} ago`
- `A weird moment from {context}`
- `I want to tell you about {name}`

**Tactical teach:**
- `Try this {timeframe}`
- `The one thing I'd do first`
- `Quick: {specific tactic}`

**Reframe:**
- `Skip the {common advice}`
- `Why "{common belief}" is keeping you stuck`
- `Forget {conventional wisdom}`

**Case study:**
- `How {name} pulled off {specific result}`
- `{Name}'s {timeframe} story`

**Behind-the-scenes:**
- `What I'm working on`
- `Real talk`
- `Behind the scenes`

**Soft offer:**
- `This is why I built it`
- `If you've been wondering...`

## Abandoned cart

**Email 1 (1 hour — friendly reminder):**
- `Did something go wrong?`
- `Your {product} is still in the cart`
- `Need help?`
- `Saved your cart`

**Email 2 (24 hours — objection + proof):**
- `About that {product}...`
- `One thing I should mention`
- `Quick question about your order`
- `Most people ask me {objection}`

**Email 3 (72 hours — final nudge):**
- `Last reminder`
- `Cart closing soon`
- `One more chance`
- `Final note`

## Launch

**Pre-launch (opportunity / transformation / mechanism):**
- `Something's shifting in {industry}`
- `What if {desired state}?`
- `The thing I've been working on`
- `Why this is different`

**Cart open:**
- `Doors are open`
- `It's live: {offer name}`
- `Here we go`
- `{Offer name} is here`

**Mid-cart:**
- `Real talk about {offer}`
- `Top questions I'm getting`
- `Here's what {name} did with it`
- `What's actually inside`

**Cart close:**
- `Closing in {N} hours`
- `Last day`
- `Final hours`
- `Doors close tonight`

**Final hours (evening of last day):**
- `Last call`
- `Closing in 3 hours`
- `Now or wait for next time`

## Evergreen

Subject patterns track to nurture + launch — the difference is pacing, not pattern. See those sections. The "first soft pitch" (email 7 of a 10-email evergreen) usually performs best with:

- `Something I've been holding back`
- `Okay, here's the thing`
- `Time to mention this`

- Email 4: `Starting in 1 hour`

- Email 6 (1 hour after): `Replay + the offer I mentioned`
- Email 7 (24 hours): `Answering the top questions from {webinar}`
- Email 8 (48 hours): `Closing tonight`

## How to use this file

1. Pick a pattern that matches the email's beat
2. Fill in the specifics
3. Run it through `/voice-check` — patterns that pass for [VOICE OWNER] go into `brains/email/winners/subjects.md`; patterns that fail get dropped
4. Generate 3 options per email — never ship the first one

The winners file overrides this file when both exist. This is the floor, the winners are the ceiling.
