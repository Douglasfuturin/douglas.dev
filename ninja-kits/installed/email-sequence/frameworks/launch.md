# Launch Sequence Framework

> **Goal:** drive the cart-open → cart-close window to the highest possible revenue. This is the only sequence where urgency is legitimate and where every email has a direct conversion target.

Two well-known frameworks dominate launch sequencing. Pick one per launch — they don't mix well.

- **Jeff Walker's Product Launch Formula (PLF)** — pre-launch teaches → opening builds anticipation → cart open → cart close. Best for: $500+ info products, courses, coaching programs. The reader gets educated for free in the pre-launch — buying becomes the natural next step.
- **Russell Brunson's Soap Opera Sequence (SOS)** — story-driven 5-email open of the cart, each email a narrative beat. Best for: lower-ticket digital products, supplements, lifestyle SKUs. Reader buys because they bonded with the story arc.

## PLF launch — 9 emails over 7 days

| # | Day | Phase | Beat | Length |
|---|-----|-------|------|--------|
| 1 | -4 | Pre-launch | **Opportunity** — name the shift in [INDUSTRY], why now matters | 400-600 words |
| 2 | -2 | Pre-launch | **Transformation** — what's possible if the reader gets this right | 350-500 words |
| 3 | -1 | Pre-launch | **Mechanism** — the unique angle / framework that delivers the transformation | 400-600 words |
| 4 | 0 | Cart open | **Doors open** — announcement, full offer, why this exists, why now | 500-800 words |
| 5 | 1 | Cart open | **FAQ + objections** — most common questions, addressed | 400-600 words |
| 6 | 3 | Mid-cart | **Case study** — one student/customer transformation with real numbers | 400-600 words |
| 7 | 5 | Mid-cart | **Behind the scenes** — why [VOICE OWNER] built this, what's inside | 350-500 words |
| 8 | 6 | Cart close | **Final 24 hours** — recap, real scarcity, decision frame | 300-450 words |
| 9 | 7 | Cart close | **Final hours** — last call, doors closing tonight, link | 150-300 words |

## SOS launch — 5 emails over 5 days (cart open period)

| # | Day | Beat | Body purpose | Length |
|---|-----|------|--------------|--------|
| 1 | 0 | **Drama / problem** | name the problem in a story — high-stakes opening | 250-400 words |
| 2 | 1 | **Wall + epiphany** | what wasn't working, then the moment things shifted | 300-450 words |
| 3 | 2 | **Hidden benefit** | a non-obvious upside the reader hasn't considered | 250-400 words |
| 4 | 3 | **Urgency + scarcity** | why now matters — real reason, not invented | 200-350 words |
| 5 | 4 | **Last call** | doors closing, final pitch, link | 150-250 words |

## Universal launch rules

- **Scarcity must be real.** Real cart-close date. Real bonus expiry. Real cohort size. Manufactured scarcity ("only 50 spots!" when there are 500) destroys trust at launch — and reputational damage compounds across future launches.
- **Open loops aggressively in pre-launch.** Email 1 references something email 3 will explain. Email 3 references something cart-open will reveal. Loops drive open rates 20-40% higher.
- **Cart-open email is the make-or-break.** This is the most-opened email of the sequence. It should re-state everything in one place — offer, price, bonuses, deadline, link — because some readers will only open this one.
- **FAQ email handles objections by name.** Read the top 5 historical objections from sales pages, DMs, and support tickets. Address them directly. Don't dance around price.
- **Last-day emails get sent twice.** Send the cart-close email in the morning AND the evening of the final day. Real open data shows 30-50% of last-day buyers buy from the evening email.
- **Tag and exclude buyers immediately.** Buyer should never receive the next email in the sequence. Connector must filter on purchase tag every send.
- **Send to non-openers separately.** Re-send the cart-open and cart-close emails with new subject lines to anyone who didn't open the original. Often doubles the revenue from a launch.

## Common failure modes

| Failure | Why it kills the launch |
|---------|------------------------|
| Pre-launch is all hype, no education | Reader can't articulate why the offer matters when cart opens |
| Cart-open email is short or buried | Single biggest revenue email of the sequence — short = lost revenue |
| FAQ email avoids the hard objections (price, time, "will it work for me") | Reader's real objection wasn't addressed → no buy |
| Scarcity is invented | One screenshot leak destroys this launch and damages the next one |
| Buyers keep getting cart-close emails | Trust collapse + refund spike |
| No non-opener resends | Leaving 30%+ of available revenue on the table |
| Last-day email sent only once | Missing the evening-buyer cohort |

## Connector requirements

For launches the connector MUST be able to:
- Filter sends by purchase tag (exclude buyers from remaining sequence)
- Send separate audience splits (openers vs. non-openers of a previous email)
- Schedule precise send times (cart-close evening email is timing-sensitive)
- Pull bonus expiry / cart-close timestamps as personalization tokens

If any of these aren't wired, flag before drafting — a half-built launch sequence is worse than no launch sequence.

## Calibration

If `brains/email/winners/launch.md` exists, look at last launch's revenue-per-email breakdown. If one specific beat (e.g., "case study email") drove 30% of revenue, weight that beat heavier in the next launch. The winners file should track this.
